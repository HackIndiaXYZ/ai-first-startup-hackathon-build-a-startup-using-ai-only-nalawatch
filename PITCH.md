# NalaWatch pitch narrative

## Problem

Every monsoon, cities across India face waterlogging, drain overflow, and emergency traffic disruption because drainage systems are aging, poorly mapped, and not monitored in real time. Citizens and civic agencies often only discover the risk after roads are flooded and public health hazards emerge.

## Solution

NalaWatch is an AI-powered urban flood intelligence platform that analyzes real-time weather, rainfall patterns, drainage capacity, geospatial data, and historical flood trends to forecast where waterlogging and drain overload are likely to occur before they reach critical levels.

## Why it matters

The cost of delayed response is high: lost productivity, damaged roads, sanitation emergencies, and lives put at risk. NalaWatch helps municipal teams and disaster responders move from reactive cleanup to proactive action.

## Product vision

- Predict flood-prone zones before the storm peaks
- Surface at-risk drains and low-lying corridors in real time
- Alert civic teams, traffic managers, and residents early
- Prioritize mitigation and emergency response actions
